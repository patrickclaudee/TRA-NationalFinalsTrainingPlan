RACE DAY STRATEGY — SUN NOV 22

Km 0–10: 6:05–6:10/km. Resist the crowd. Slower than target on purpose.
Km 10–21: Lock into 5:55–5:58/km. Controlled cruise. Gel every 6km, salt at 10km.
Km 21–32: Maintain 5:55. Caffeine gel at 22km. Race starts here.
Km 32–42: Sub-4:10 is yours if you hit 32km by about 3:09. Empty the tank.

Nutrition: Carbs/gel every 6km. Salt at 10km & 20km.
Race Shoe: 361 Flame 5  ·  Sub-4:10 = 5:55/km. Memorize it.

Key Principles

MP In Long Runs

Your legs need to know 5:55/km at km 20+. That contrast wins the second half.

Easy Means Easy

Easy portions at 6:30–6:45+. Run them faster and the MP segments fall apart.

5:55/km Is Your Anchor

By race day it should feel boring. If it feels hard, you went out too fast.

Knee Rule

Flare = repeat the week. Never push through lateral knee pain.

Peak Then Absorb

The 3-week taper is where the Nov 1 long run turns into fitness.

Taper Is Intentional

Flat, restless legs are correct. Don't add junk miles.
